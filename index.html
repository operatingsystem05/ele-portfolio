About me

A self-taught photographer based in Brussels, with over eight years of experience capturing vivid and authentic moments. Specializing in concert, event, and cultural scene photography, I have developed a committed and inclusive perspective on the Belgian cultural scene.

My career is distinguished, among other things, by a significant collaboration with Vice Belgium, where I sensitively documented the social and artistic realities of the capital. My work has also been featured at major events such as the Listen Festival, showcasing my ability to capture the raw energy of musical and festive gatherings.

Beyond these institutional collaborations, I have actively worked with various cultural actors in Brussels. My approach centers on three fundamental pillars: the representation of women and queer people, and the exploration of underground culture. Through my lens, I document the resilience, creativity, and unique expressions that animate the fringes of Brussels’ cultural scene, transforming each shot into an act of documentation and celebration.

My portfolio reflects this constant quest for truth and inclusion, offering a faithful mirror of the vitality and complexity of the subjects I photograph.
